The catalog contains nine mechanism classes from six donors, accumulated during
development: eight human-given targets and one model-selected target following
same-source development. This is not a 9/9 extraction rate, unseen-source
discovery result, or evidence of stable composition advantage.

The representative replay supports scrutiny of declared SEARCH/REPLACE
semantics. The paper's complete host and benchmark claims require their
separate original records; this small candidate does not reproduce them.
