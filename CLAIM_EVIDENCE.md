# Claim-to-evidence guide

The levels below are deliberately distinct: **executable** means this bundle
contains an offline entry for a disclosed local relation; **recorded** means
an inspectable historical receipt or explicit field extract, not a fresh run;
**reported** identifies conclusions in a dated analysis rather than a raw receipt; **indexed** means
only provenance is supplied; **restricted** inputs are not released. A report
of acceptance is not an independent reproduction of every acceptance test.
