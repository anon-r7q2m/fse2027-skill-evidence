# Claim-to-evidence guide

The levels below are deliberately distinct: **executable** means this bundle
contains an offline entry for a disclosed local relation; **recorded** means
an inspectable historical receipt or explicit field extract, not a fresh run;
**reported** identifies conclusions in a dated analysis rather than a raw receipt; **indexed** means
only provenance is supplied; **restricted** inputs are not released. A report
of acceptance is not an independent reproduction of every acceptance test.

## Selection, roles and chronology

`principles/case_analysis.md` and `study/dependency_cases.md` retain the
retrospective source frame, version-specific observations, complete GP/feedback/PQ
panels and positive cases. The explanatory six-family selection was purposive
and retrospective. Coding agents drafted and cross-checked fields under the
researcher's direction. This is not independent human coding or a blinded
adjudication study. Unknown properties remain unassessed; later diagnostic
requirements do not retroactively change old contracts.

The dependency matrix was assembled before capacity score release. Its pending
entry is preserved as history; `capacity/` holds the subsequent released result.
All study populations and denominators remain separate.
