# Consumer decisions: a cross-case analysis

This synthesis connects existing source traces, recorded executions, and the
closed prospective study. It was assembled after the observations. The four
findings below are not the original P1–P3 guidance, a new preregistered test,
a prevalence estimate, or evidence of guidance superiority.

The unit of analysis is the relationship between a justified requirement,
the relevant object or configuration, and the component that accepts,
selects, continues, stops, or reports. Eight rows below are explanatory
families with different designs; the Pro row contains three synthetic cases.

## Four conditional findings

1. **A shared checking requirement can align repair and acceptance around the same error.**
   When a generated assertion plays both roles, their agreement is not
   independent support for that assertion. Check its public authority and
   preserved behavior before strengthening enforcement. The observed feedback
   regression is code-level; all four policies retain the same 4/7 solved set
   and one unscored task.
2. **Transformations can break evidence transfer in opposite directions.**
   GP removes candidate distinctions before checking; smolagents damages a
   correct answer before a correct check. Compare original and received
   objects and the property preserved by the transformation. Legitimate test
   restoration and declared subset reports remain legitimate.
3. **Visible state is not necessarily state used by a decision.**
   Configuration eligibility, response events and rendered status require
   different delivery checks. Inspect the actual consumer input, not only the
   log or stream. Policy authority is distinct from observational evidence.
4. **Plausible output does not establish that a declared check ran.**
   A valid-answer control can look correct even when validation was removed.
   An applicable rejecting input and invocation record distinguish output
   validity from active validation.

An operational sequence is to name the consumer and its justified requirement,
check participation/configuration, compare original and received objects,
inspect the receiving representation, and test the smallest justified
correction with a legal control. This procedure is a synthesis, not a proven
optimal algorithm or measured improvement over ordinary diagnosis. Stop at the
observed endpoint; task benefit needs separate grading.

and loading them through the same component API:

[CodeExecutorAgent's example](https://github.com/microsoft/autogen/blob/027ecf0a379bcc1d09956d46d12d44a3ad9cee14/python/packages/autogen-agentchat/src/autogen_agentchat/agents/_code_executor_agent.py#L342)

are public native features. The measured interaction affects continuation
under a messages/events limit at response boundaries. It is not a monetary

## Task utility and measurement remain separate

The [complete capacity outcomes](../capacity/README.md) and
[new existing-record witnesses](../capacity/score_witnesses/README.md) retain
all original denominators and scores. Ten primary naive-composition paths
stopped at the first local input limit before any model request; one stopped
on an available nonempty .pyx file. These explain installed-policy reachability,
not a new failure rate or the benefit of an unexecuted repair.

The [audit resource](../audit/README.md) retains scoped methodology and
aggregate remediation proposals over heterogeneous units. Its size and role
separation do not calibrate semantic validity. This synthesis does not create
an authoritative blacklist or change any frozen experimental cohort.
