# Retrospective case map

`cases.json` records source identities, target profiles, six dependency fields,
and project-relative evidence references. It includes successful transfers,
rejected proposals, interface failures, historical positive cohorts, completed
composition blocks, missing outcomes, and excluded incomplete histories.

The linked original reports are not all bundled in this representative replay.
Their paths identify provenance in the research repository; this map does not
reproduce their experiments or certify their outcomes independently. No private
annotation, hidden evaluator case, or live scoring record is included.

The matrix is a purposive retrospective synthesis. Its rows are not independent
samples for estimating extraction success, failure prevalence, or scaling laws.
Package versions, discovery cells, task blocks, shared candidate policies and
solver/scorer dispositions retain separate units. The complete released
capacity matrix and its primary-only participation/usage summary are included
under `../capacity/`; their benchmark outcomes are separate from the case map
and the offline replays.
