## Question and permissible conclusion

Can two new AI analysis contexts apply an existing, unchanged method view to
four previously unused external decision units, producing evidence-supported
actions, appropriate retention of legal behavior, or justified withholding?
Record errors, missing evidence and disagreement as outcomes. This is an
author-operated, bounded transfer-use exercise. It is not human usability,
external adoption, defect prevalence, a comparison with ordinary debugging,
live-agent performance, an independent model-family comparison or a benchmark
score. There is no untreated baseline and no causal claim of benefit from the
worksheet. Two contexts may share a backend and training; eight responses are
not eight independent external incidents.

## Frozen record-level adjudication

The seven worksheet fields define the actor record. Do not add a new causal
taxonomy or require new case-specific words after seeing output. For each of
the fixed eight context-by-unit cells, preserve the original answer and report:

1. Completion and boundary/budget status, including partial or absent output.
2. Obligation/domain and authority judgment, supported versus assumed or
   unknown, with its actual cited evidence.
3. Recommended action: `SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`,
   `INSUFFICIENTLY_JUSTIFIED` or `REFERENCE_UNRESOLVED`, with a brief reason.
4. Whether references support the decisive premises, and whether legal
   behavior is retained or wrongly forbidden.
5. Supported coexisting contributors preserved, unsupported causal certainty,
   and discriminating missing observations identified or overlooked.
6. The action's observed, proposed or externally reported intervention status;
   record a false tested-success claim separately from the recommended action.

`SUPPORTED` requires both an admissible action and its necessary premises;
matching an action label alone is insufficient. `CONTRADICTED` requires a
concrete conflict with applicable packet evidence/authority. A plausible but
unsupported answer is `INSUFFICIENTLY_JUSTIFIED`, not automatically a known
wrong action. A different evidence-supported action can be acceptable without
matching a maintainer patch. Retention and withholding can be correct, but
blanket abstention on sufficient evidence is not automatically successful.
Preserve partial correctness and explicit alternatives rather than treating
every contributor not named as the same kind of error.

Report all eight planned records and their resolvable subset separately.
Unknown reference items are not failures or known-correct answers. Partial and
missing actor records remain in the planned table. Report disagreements and
agreement descriptively, including agreement on error. Do not use majority
vote as truth, statistical significance, population accuracy, schema fill
rate as scientific success, model ranking or presumed effort savings.
