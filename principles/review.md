## Claim disposition

The evidence supports three conditional engineering principles and the stated
local actions. It does not establish an independently predictive theory,
minimal dependency closure, extra utility of generic scope metadata, unique
superiority of caller-path testing, a leaderboard error rate, or benchmark
improvement from the new diagnostics. Synthetic conditions and reused objects
are not independent task samples. The positive controls are retained.

The GP and Pro groups used no implementation fix. Feedback used its one
permitted correction and retains both the initial runner/result and the final
ones. The version is closed at its planned cap; no additional fixture or
model/benchmark cohort is triggered by this review.
