Additional analysis guide:
P1. Before choosing a boundary, trace the intended consumer's required producers, ownership and configuration; identify what must accompany an adaptation.
P2. Compare the checked object and asserted property with the exact claim the downstream decision needs. A legitimate projection/subset may remain useful; require property correspondence, not object identity for its own sake.
P3. Derive controls from the actual transformed input path and justified obligation. Retain correct behavior outside the requested adaptation. Check whether the original object, obligation or delivery path needs a local change.
These are questions, not guarantees of a defect or superior diagnosis. A direct check may suffice; retain that explanation.
