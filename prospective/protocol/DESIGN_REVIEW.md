# Design review before implementation

Two in-product reviewers, adversarial_f38_methods and adversarial_f38_novelty,
reviewed the design without opening target implementations or results. Both
support the bounded study after the following corrections, now in PROTOCOL.md:

- Independent public obligation/oracle; termination or a wrong scripted answer
  alone is not a framework defect.
- Equal strong baseline, fresh context per family/arm, recorded allocation and
  opportunity budget; one trajectory cannot establish general method advantage.
- Fixed model scripts shared by all variants, native consumer, explicit script
  exhaustion/shape mismatch INCONCLUSIVE, actual trusted local execution.
- Union of both inputs on original and separate patches; all legal controls;
  at most two fixture-wiring corrections with unchanged semantic predictions.
- No outcome-based target replacement, no new samples to chase positive results;
  known-source exposure and full-workflow versus slicing are distinguished.
- Call patches predeclared local interventions; do not claim proven minimality.

Neither review supplied an acceptance score or a guarantee of a positive result.
The user authorization dated September29 supersedes the prior decision to stop
at manuscript-only revision for this new, separately named finite study.
